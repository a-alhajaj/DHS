Hi, I’m **AlJood**, and this is my first *Data and Human Space* project.

For this assignment, I mapped three GeoNames feature codes across China:  
**MN** for *mines*, **MLM** for *ore treatment plants*, and **PRT** for *ports*.

Together, these three features create a simple way of looking at the movement from **extraction → processing → circulation** across the country.

> **What can these three kinds of places tell us about China’s infrastructure, and what might the dataset be leaving out?**

My argument is that the map reveals useful spatial patterns, but it is **not a complete picture** of China’s mineral landscape. What appears on the map depends on how GeoNames classifies places, what gets recorded, and what remains missing.
