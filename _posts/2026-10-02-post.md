I Chose China Because...

Part of the reason was personal. I had just come back from studying away in Shanghai, and I missed it. Shanghai became my entry point into thinking about China more broadly. Even its name, 上海 (Shànghǎi), combines 上 (shàng), “above/on,” and 海 (hǎi), “sea.” Spending a semester there made me much more curious about what exists beyond the cities themselves: where materials come from, how industries are connected, and how goods move across such a huge country.
