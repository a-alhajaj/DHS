Hi, I’m **Aljood**, and this is my first *Data and Human Space* project.

For this assignment, I am mapping China through three types of places recorded in GeoNames: **mines, ore treatment plants, and ports**. At first glance, they are simply points scattered across a map. But together, they offer a way of thinking about what happens before materials move through wider networks of trade: where they are extracted, where they are processed, and where they are transported.

Together, we will ask: **How does GeoNames represent mines, ore treatment plants, and ports across China, and what can the patterns and gaps in this data tell us?**
